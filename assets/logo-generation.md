## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished original logo symbol for PrivacyDesk, a Windows privacy utility. A clever visual play on the Windows four-pane window and making yourself hidden: four crisp geometric window panes form a single compact emblem, with an anonymous cloaked/hooded silhouette ingeniously cut out of the center using negative space. The silhouette is abstract and discreet, not a scary hacker or detailed face. Let a small section of the right panes disappear into two or three clean geometric fragments to suggest invisibility. Strong simple silhouette, balanced, professional desktop-app brand, readable at small icon sizes. Flat vector-like precision, restrained mint teal #68E0C4 and deep navy #142239 palette matching a dark privacy app. Single centered standalone icon, ample transparent margin, true transparent background. No text, no letters, no wordmark, no mockup, no shadows, no glow, no gradients, no 3D, no watermark. It should reference a four-pane window while being clearly its own inventive privacy emblem.
